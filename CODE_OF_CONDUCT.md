# Code of conduct

Ex Machinis is a game for learning, and people learn best where it's safe to get things wrong. Everyone who takes part (players, contributors and maintainers, in the game, on GitHub, and in our community spaces) is asked to:

- **Be kind and patient.** Many people here are new to programming. Explain, don't belittle.
- **Criticise code and ideas, not people.**
- **Assume good faith,** and ask before assuming.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't share other people's personal details.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual content or attention, threats, and deliberately disrupting other people's play or discussions. (Out-competing someone's drones in the game is fair play.)

## Reporting

Report problems to the maintainers privately: by [GitHub's private reporting](../../security/advisories/new) if the problem is on GitHub, or the contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or block people who break this code, in proportion to what happened: usually a warning first, a ban for serious or repeated harm.
