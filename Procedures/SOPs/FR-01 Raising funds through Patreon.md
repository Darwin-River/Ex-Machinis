# FR-01: Raising funds through Patreon

1. # <a id="1"></a>Purpose

   1. <a id="1.1"></a>This standard operating procedure (SOP) sets out how the project raises money through [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/exmachinis) to keep Ex Machinis free for students and the general public: how patrons are kept informed, how their feedback shapes the game, how they are thanked, and how the project reports what their money pays for.

2. # <a id="2"></a>Roles and responsibilities

   1. <a id="2.1"></a>The project lead, for Darwin River Software LLC, owns the Patreon page, sets its tiers and benefits, publishes every patron post, handles all money, and decides how funds are spent.

   2. <a id="2.2"></a>Claude drafts the monthly patron update and other patron posts when directed, using the "Machine instructions for drafting the monthly patron update" ([FR-01-M1](../Attachments/FR-01-M1%20Machine%20instructions%20for%20drafting%20the%20monthly%20patron%20update.md)) and the "Monthly patron update template" ([FR-01-T1](../Attachments/FR-01-T1%20Monthly%20patron%20update%20template.md)). Claude has no access to Patreon, to patrons' details, or to money.

   3. <a id="2.3"></a>Developers give the project lead material for updates: what they shipped and what is coming.

3. # <a id="3"></a>Required resources

   1. <a id="3.1"></a>The Patreon page, [patreon.com/exmachinis](https://www.patreon.com/exmachinis), and its account, held by the project lead.

   2. <a id="3.2"></a>`.github/FUNDING.yml`, which shows the Sponsor button on GitHub, and the "Our Awesome Sponsors" section of `README.md`.

4. # <a id="4"></a>General guidelines

   1. <a id="4.1"></a>This SOP will be formatted and maintained IAW [QA-01](QA-01%20Writing%20and%20maintaining%20Ex%20Machinis%20standard%20operating%20procedures.md).

   2. <a id="4.2"></a>Patrons support the mission; they do not buy an advantage. The game and its teaching materials stay free and fair for everyone. Patron benefits may include thanks, early looks at what is coming, a say in what is built next, and time with the developers, but never anything that helps a patron's drones in the game.

   3. <a id="4.3"></a>Be open about money. Tell patrons, at least once a quarter, roughly what their support paid for, such as hosting, development time and outreach to schools.

   4. <a id="4.4"></a>Ask, don't nag. The pitch is the mission: a free place where high school students and anyone else can learn to code. Most posts on every channel should teach or show rather than ask, per [OR-02](OR-02%20Engaging%20on%20social%20media.md) §4.4.

   5. <a id="4.5"></a>Keep patrons' personal details private. Thank a patron by name in public only if they have agreed to it.

   6. <a id="4.6"></a>Other ways of raising money, such as education grants, school sponsorships or donations from companies, are proposed in an issue and agreed by the project lead before anyone pursues them. When one is adopted, add an SOP for it in the FR category.

5. # <a id="5"></a>Procedures

   1. <a id="5.1"></a>Each month, the project lead directs Claude to draft the patron update, per [FR-01-M1](../Attachments/FR-01-M1%20Machine%20instructions%20for%20drafting%20the%20monthly%20patron%20update.md). Claude posts the draft in an issue titled "Patron update: {month year}", labeled `patreon`.

   2. <a id="5.2"></a>The project lead edits and publishes the update on Patreon, comments on the issue with its link, and closes the issue.

   3. <a id="5.3"></a>Patrons' feedback and requests that come in through Patreon are brought into GitHub by the project lead as issues, per [AD-01](AD-01%20Creating%20and%20resolving%20GitHub%20issues.md), without the patron's name unless they agree. Patrons who want to can also post issues themselves.

   4. <a id="5.4"></a>Invite patrons into playtest rounds where they want to play, per [DV-02](DV-02%20Running%20playtest%20rounds.md), and share the round's results with them.

   5. <a id="5.5"></a>Keep the "Our Awesome Sponsors" section of `README.md` current with the patrons and sponsors who have agreed to be named, through a pull request, per [AD-02](AD-02%20Working%20with%20branches%20and%20pull%20requests.md).

   6. <a id="5.6"></a>Once a quarter, include in the patron update what patron support paid for, and what the project hopes to do next with more support, such as a new lesson series or a bigger server for classrooms.

   7. <a id="5.7"></a>Point people to Patreon from the places they already meet the project: the website, the blog ([OR-01](OR-01%20Writing%20and%20publishing%20blog%20posts.md)) and social media ([OR-02](OR-02%20Engaging%20on%20social%20media.md)).

   8. <a id="5.8"></a>Review the Patreon tiers and benefits once a year, or when patron feedback asks for it, in an issue. Check every benefit against §4.2.
