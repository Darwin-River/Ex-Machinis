# OR-02-T1: Social media post template

Use one block per post in a "Social posts" issue. Replace each instruction in curly brackets.

~~~~markdown
### Post {n}: {channel}, suggested for {day}

**Purpose:** {teach / show / announce / thank / support}

**Text:**

> {The post, within the channel's length limit. Lead with what the reader sees or learns. One link.}

**Link:** {the game, a blog post, a doc, or Patreon}

**Image or snippet:** {what to attach, such as a drone page screenshot or a few lines of Lua, or "none"}

**Source:** {the PR, issue, round or post this comes from}
~~~~
