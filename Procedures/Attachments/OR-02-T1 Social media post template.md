# OR-02-T1: Social media post template

Use one block per post in a "Social posts" issue. Replace each instruction in curly brackets. New blog posts are announced automatically and need no block, per [OR-02](../SOPs/OR-02%20Engaging%20on%20social%20media.md) §5.2.

~~~~markdown
### Post {n}: {channel: Bluesky, Mastodon, Reddit (which community), itch.io}, suggested for {day}

**Purpose:** {teach / show / announce / thank / support (at most once a month)}

**Text:**

> {The post, within the channel's length limit (Bluesky 300 characters, Mastodon 500). Lead with what the reader sees or learns. One link.}

**Link:** {the game, a blog post, a doc, a drone's page, or the support page}

**Image or snippet:** {what to attach, such as a drone page screenshot or a few lines of Lua, or "none"}

**Source:** {the PR, issue, round or post this comes from}
~~~~
