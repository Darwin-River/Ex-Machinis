# Balance report

Generated by `tools/balance/static_check.py` from the game data (#961): do not edit by hand. After changing `game-engine/sql/game-data/` or `tools/balance/objectives.json`, run `python3 tools/balance/static_check.py` and commit this file. The process is [DV-03](../Procedures/SOPs/DV-03%20Tuning%20gameplay%20parameters.md).

The figures are a static model at the House's listed prices: no booms or slumps, no import price, no daily demand caps and no crowding. They show the shape a change gives the economy. `tools/balance/measure_live.py` and the playtest rounds say what players actually get.

A **guard** fails the build when it is broken. A **target** is reported until it is met, then promoted to a guard.

### The production ladder (PO-05 to PO-07)

**One step**: buy a recipe's inputs from the House, make it, and sell it to the House's best bid. **Whole chain**: make it all the way from refined goods bought from the House (far goods the House doesn't sell are priced at their value with its markup). Both are in credits a tool-hour.

### Distance and the frontier (PO-13, PO-14)

- A trip burns 5 fuel to the Moon and 5 to Neptune.
- Far haul: a 1,000-cargo hull with the Mining Kit and 100 fuel aboard has room for 604 refined units. Earth to Jupiter at their closest takes 99.5 game hours each way at 19.6 m/s².
- Best far mineral: Zenolite (N) at abundance 10, 151 batches a trip, 621.7 game hours with the trips, 30.2 credits a game hour at the House's best bids.
